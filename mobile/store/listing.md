## Short description (80)

## Full description
Sihha AI brings your care into one app, in Arabic or English.
• Chat with an AI health assistant and get a clear next step — self-care, a visit, or urgent care
• Book your doctor's open slots instantly
• Track medications, get reminders, and see when a refill is due
• Identify a pill from a photo
• Read your visit summaries and prescriptions from your doctor
• Find partner pharmacies that stock your medicine
• Manage family members' health from one account

Sihha AI supports — and never replaces — your doctor. In an emergency call 997.
